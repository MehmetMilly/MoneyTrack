Create one high-resolution product concept board for a bilingual personal finance app called MoneyTrack.

Format:
- One single image, 16:9 landscape, clean white gallery background.
- A 2x3 grid of six polished app screenshots, all from the same visual system.
- Mix desktop and mobile surfaces so the visual direction can be judged across the product.
- Use realistic UI mockups, not marketing hero art.

Screens to include:
1. Desktop main dashboard: available cash, goals, debts, installments, upcoming reminders, and a monthly trend.
2. Mobile home dashboard in Arabic RTL.
3. Add transaction / obligation form with account, amount, date, category, and notes.
4. Savings goal detail screen for a tablet purchase, with progress, remaining amount, and planned contributions.
5. Installment timeline screen for recurring obligations and in-app reminders.
6. Settings / profile screen with language switch EN/AR, currency SAR, theme, and account controls.

Visual direction:
Quiet luxury finance. Make it feel like a premium private banking tool redesigned for personal daily use. Use a refined palette of pearl white, charcoal ink, muted graphite, deep green-black, warm metallic gold accents, and small signals of Saudi Riyal currency. Typography should feel editorial and controlled: large confident numbers, compact labels, elegant spacing, no noisy dashboards. Surfaces should feel expensive but not flashy: matte panels, hairline dividers, subtle paper-like texture, restrained shadows, precise chart lines, and premium mobile interaction states.

UX and content requirements:
- The app is for personal tracking: assets/accounts, expenses, debts, installments, savings goals, and in-app reminders.
- It supports full English and Arabic UI switching; show at least one Arabic RTL screen and one English LTR screen.
- Currency should be SAR, shown naturally in the UI.
- Use believable finance data, not fake crypto or stock-trading content.
- The app should feel functional and ready to build, with real controls and readable hierarchy.

Motion implication:
Suggest fluid, premium motion through layered cards in mid-transition, subtle progress-ring states, and smooth mobile sheet transitions, but keep the image itself a static UI concept.

Avoid:
- Generic SaaS gradient blobs, purple-blue AI dashboards, glassmorphism overload, fake stock charts, crypto vibes, neon cyberpunk, decorative 3D coins, giant hero copy, and unreadable tiny text.
- Do not make it look like a landing page. This must look like the actual app.

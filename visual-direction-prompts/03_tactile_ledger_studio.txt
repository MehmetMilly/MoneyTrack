Create one high-resolution product concept board for a bilingual personal finance app called MoneyTrack.

Format:
- One single image, 16:9 landscape.
- Six screenshots arranged in a precise 2x3 grid.
- Show a consistent design system across desktop and mobile.
- Screens should appear as app UI screenshots with minimal device framing.

Screens to include:
1. Desktop dashboard with balance, accounts, goal progress, debts, installments, and reminders.
2. Mobile Arabic RTL home screen.
3. New entry form for transaction, debt, or installment.
4. Goal detail screen with contribution plan and target date.
5. Debt and installment timeline with paid/upcoming states.
6. Settings screen with profile, language switch, theme, currency SAR, and backup/sync status.

Visual direction:
Tactile ledger studio. Blend the discipline of a personal accounting notebook with modern app polish. The UI should feel physical, organized, and handcrafted: soft off-white canvas, ink-black text, sage and muted blue accents, thin ruled lines, tabbed sections, ledger rows, lightly embossed surfaces, small stamps/status marks, and carefully spaced forms. It should not look old-fashioned; it should look like a premium digital notebook for money clarity.

UX and content requirements:
- Personal finance focus: manual entries, assets, expenses, debts, installments, savings goals, and in-app reminders.
- Include English and Arabic, with at least one RTL Arabic screen.
- Currency is SAR.
- Use readable row layouts, editable fields, goal progress, and due-date indicators.
- Make the interface feel calm, useful, and personal.

Motion implication:
Imply motion with tab transitions, expanding ledger rows, animated checkmarks for paid obligations, and a smooth add-entry drawer.

Avoid:
- Scrapbook clutter, fake handwritten illegibility, excessive beige monotone, old paper gimmicks, random stickers, generic finance cards, crypto charts, neon gradients, and decorative objects that are not part of the UI.

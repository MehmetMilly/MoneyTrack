Create one high-resolution product concept board for a bilingual personal finance app called MoneyTrack.

Format:
- One single image, 16:9 landscape.
- A disciplined 2x3 grid of six app screenshots.
- Include desktop and mobile screens with consistent spacing, typography, color, and component behavior.
- Make the result look like a real app design review board.

Screens to include:
1. Desktop overview dashboard: accounts, available cash, goals, debts, installments, reminders, and month summary.
2. Mobile Arabic RTL dashboard.
3. Add/edit financial item form.
4. Goal detail screen with progress and contribution schedule.
5. Obligations table/timeline with debts and installments.
6. Settings/profile screen with language EN/AR, SAR currency, sync, and account controls.

Visual direction:
Swiss utility premium. The interface should feel precise, fast, and deeply usable: white and cool-gray backgrounds, black typography, sharp hierarchy, exact grid, compact tables, segmented controls, clear status color, and sparse high-quality icons. Use a small accent palette: emerald for positive/funded, red-orange for overdue, blue for selected controls, graphite for neutral states. It should feel like Linear or a high-end finance operations tool adapted for personal money tracking, not a consumer bank clone.

UX and content requirements:
- Designed for daily use: quick scan, quick add, quick edit.
- Full EN/AR language switching; include one proper Arabic RTL mobile screen.
- SAR currency.
- Manual entries, debts, installments, goals, reminders, and actionable summaries.
- Make desktop information-dense and mobile ergonomic.

Motion implication:
Suggest precise product motion: rows expanding inline, command menu opening, chart hover states, fast segmented-control transitions, and subtle paid-state confirmations.

Avoid:
- Generic card grids, soft startup gradients, decorative illustrations, giant rounded pills everywhere, fake investment dashboards, lifestyle imagery, playful mascots, and excessive shadows.
